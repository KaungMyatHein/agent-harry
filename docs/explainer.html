How the team works

An interactive family tree of the agents — pick a use case and the team rearranges.

Each of the 20 sub-agents has one job and a fixed place in the lifecycle. But the shape of the team changes depending on what you're running: a fresh feature pulls in everyone, an audit only needs a tight cluster, a handoff is a straight assembly line. Click any node to see what it does, who it talks to, and the Stop Gate that follows.

# PM Launch Architect

`pm-launch-architect` · **Phase:** Deliver · **Model:** sonnet · **Decision authority:** propose

### Hooks
- **Hook A —** "Designed and ready" is where most products quietly stall — built, beautiful, and in front of no one.
- **Hook B —** A beachhead segment, an ICP, the right motions, and a launch sequence — the path from finished design to in-market and growing.
- **Hook C —** Who's the one specific group you launch to first — and what's the pre-mortem that tells you how this goes wrong before it does?

## TL;DR
`pm-launch-architect` takes something from "designed and ready" to "in market and growing." It's a pragmatic GTM lead that names the beachhead instead of the dream — picking the first segment and ICP, choosing sales/marketing motions, writing battlecards, designing growth loops, running pre-mortems, and sequencing the launch.

## What it does
It owns the gap between a finished design and an actual market. The question it answers: how do we get this to the right users without setting cash on fire?

It names the beachhead, not the dream — choosing the specific first segment to win and defining the ideal customer profile (ICP), instead of trying to be for everyone at once.

It works the go-to-market mechanics: the sales and marketing motions that fit, battlecards for competing, and growth loops that compound rather than leak.

It pressure-tests with pre-mortems — imagining the launch already failed and working backwards — and lays out launch sequencing so the steps happen in a sane order.

It proposes the GTM plan. You decide what to commit to.

## When to reach for it
- Something is designed and ready, and the question becomes "how do we actually get this to market?"
- You need to pick a beachhead segment and define an ICP instead of launching to everyone.
- You want GTM motions, battlecards, growth loops, a pre-mortem, or a launch sequence — without burning cash.

## Inputs → Outputs
| It needs | It produces |
|---|---|
| A designed, ready offering plus what's known about market, users, and competitors | Beachhead segment, ICP, sales/marketing motions, battlecards, growth loops, a pre-mortem, and a launch sequence |

## Where it sits in the pipeline
It runs after the Define and Deliver phases — once the product is designed and ready — when the work shifts from "is it good?" to "how do we get it to the right users without burning cash?" It turns the validated design into a market plan.

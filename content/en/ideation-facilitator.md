# Ideation Facilitator

`ideation-facilitator` · **Phase:** Define · **Model:** sonnet · **Decision authority:** autonomous

### Hooks
- **Hook A —** You jumped straight to wireframes and shipped the first idea you had. The good one never got a chance.
- **Hook B —** Walk in with one problem, walk out with twelve ways to solve it — then pick the strongest on purpose.
- **Hook C —** What if the best version of your feature is an idea you haven't let yourself think yet?

## TL;DR
The ideation-facilitator opens up the funnel before you narrow it down. It runs divergent ideation — reframing the problem, generating lots of concepts fast, and pushing you past the obvious first answer. You leave with a spread of options to choose from instead of one default.

## What it does
It keeps the thinking generative. Instead of converging on the first workable idea, it deliberately produces many — so the concept you eventually choose is a choice, not an accident.

It reframes the problem using "How Might We" prompts, turning a stuck statement into several openings worth exploring. From there it runs rapid, crazy-8s-style generation: lots of rough concepts, fast, with no early judgment to kill the weird ones.

It hands you sketch prompts and concept directions you can react to — the raw material a designer needs to feel the space before committing. The whole point is breadth first, so convergence later is informed.

It works autonomously: you give it a solid problem frame, and it drives the divergent round on its own, then brings the spread back for you to react to.

## When to reach for it
- The problem is framed and understood, but you have not explored solutions yet.
- You keep defaulting to the first idea and want real alternatives on the table.
- You need "How Might We" reframes to break a stuck problem statement open.

## Inputs → Outputs
| It needs | It produces |
|---|---|
| A solid problem frame (the "what" and "who," not a solution) | A set of "How Might We" reframes |
| The user's goal or job to be done | A spread of distinct concept directions and sketch prompts to choose from |

## Where it sits in the pipeline
It runs in the Define phase, after problem framing is solid but before any wireframing. It is the divergent step that feeds the convergent ones: once you pick a concept here, the lo-fi-designer takes that chosen direction into layout. Reach for it before you let anyone sketch a screen.

✦ What it is
The discovery-researcher is the agent that refuses to skip ahead to solutions. It turns raw research — interviews, surveys, analytics, secondary sources, old artifacts — into a clearly framed problem you can actually build against.

✦ When to use it
When you're starting a new feature and want the real problem before anyone sketches. Or when stakeholders disagree about what the problem even is.

✦ Why use it
Without it, a vague "users are unhappy" never becomes a sharp, testable statement of who hurts, when, and why — and the loudest opinion wins instead of the evidence. It runs JTBD discovery to surface the job people are really hiring your product to do.

✦ What it is
design-engineer turns an approved lo-fi layout into a real, runnable frontend prototype written in your project's actual stack — not a throwaway mockup.

✦ When to use it
A lo-fi layout just got approved and you want a clickable prototype in your real stack, not a static image. Or you need to pressure-test every state before anyone commits to build.

Without it, you only ever see the happy path. It wires all five states — empty, loading, populated, error, and edge — as toggle-able routes, filled with dummy data, so "what does this look like while loading?" is one click away instead of imagination.

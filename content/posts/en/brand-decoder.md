Most "off-brand" work isn't ugly — it just misread how the brand thinks about itself. By then it's already in review. 🧬

✦ What it is
brand-decoder reads how a company already thinks about itself instead of stamping a generic framework on top. A brand interpreter, not a brand imposer.

✦ When to use it
You're doing client work or a design test inside a brand you don't own. Or the product is established and you must design within its existing identity.

✦ Why use it
Without it, downstream positioning, IA, and visual work build on a guess and read as "not us." It decodes the brand's concept, worldview, mental model, vocabulary, and on/off-brand tells into brand-concept.md before a single screen is drawn.

✦ How to use it
Just tell Agent Harry:
"Use the brand-decoder agent to decode this product's brand into a brand-concept.md before we start positioning."

✦ Benefits
- Surfaces the mental model the brand never wrote down
- Captures the vocabulary it uses — and the words it avoids
- A reusable brand-concept.md every later phase reads, so work lands native from the first pass

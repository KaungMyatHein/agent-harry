Five stakeholders, five different answers to "what is this product?" That's not alignment — that's a launch about to wobble. 🎯

✦ What it is
The product-positioner sharpens what a product, feature, or release IS and ISN'T. It picks a side: clear positioning statements, value props, a differentiation narrative, and naming — instead of mushy "all things to all people" messaging.

✦ When to use it
When stakeholders are pulled in different directions about scope or message. Or when you need to define not just what a release IS, but what it deliberately ISN'T.

✦ Why use it
The fastest way to make a product forgettable is to make it for everyone. Without sharp positioning, the market remembers you for nothing in particular instead of something specific.

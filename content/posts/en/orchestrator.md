You don't have a design problem. You have a sequencing problem — and it's why your sprint keeps stalling. 🧭

✦ What it is
The orchestrator is the planning and routing brain of Agent Harry. You hand it a messy, multi-phase goal and it breaks it down, picks the right sub-agents, sequences them, and drops in approval gates. It plans and delegates — it never does the design work itself.

✦ When to use it
When you're staring at a multi-phase task and don't know the right order. Or when you have a deadline ("ship X by Y") and need a realistic, gated path to get there.

✦ Why use it
Without it, your sub-agents fire blindly and return a pile of disconnected docs. The orchestrator runs an Alignment Loop — diagnose, smallest next move, realign — so the plan stays honest as reality shifts.

✦ How to use it
Just tell Agent Harry:
"Use the orchestrator agent to plan a discovery-to-deliver cycle for our new onboarding flow, shipping in three weeks."

✦ Benefits
- One sequenced plan with the right agents in the right order
- Enforced Research-First and Success-Metrics gates
- Synthesized outputs — one next move, not a document dump

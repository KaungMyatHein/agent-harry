✦ What it is
critique-partner is Agent Harry's built-in devil's advocate. Hand it any artifact and it stress-tests it for weak logic, lazy assumptions, and comfortable consensus — direct, respectful, run between phases.

✦ When to use it
You want to stress-test an agent's output before it moves to the next phase. Or something feels "too clean" and you can't say why — that instinct is worth running down.

✦ How to use it

✦ What it is
The ideation-facilitator opens up the funnel before you narrow it down. It runs divergent ideation — reframing the problem, generating lots of concepts fast, and pushing you past the obvious first answer.

✦ When to use it
When the problem is framed and understood, but you haven't explored solutions yet. Or when you keep defaulting to the first idea and want real alternatives on the table.

✦ Why use it
Without it, the concept you ship is an accident, not a choice. It runs "How Might We" reframes and rapid crazy-8s-style generation with no early judgment — so the weird, possibly-best ideas survive long enough to be considered.

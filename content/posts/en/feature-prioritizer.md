Everything's a P0. Which means nothing is — and your team is drowning in a backlog nobody trusts. 📊

✦ What it is
The feature-prioritizer is the agent that refuses to call everything a P0. Hand it a list of features, ideas, or scope items and it decides what to build, in what order, and what to cut — using RICE, ICE, and Kano scoring.

✦ When to use it
When you have a pile of features and need to decide order, scope, or cuts. Or when you're in a scope argument with stakeholders and need a defensible, scored answer.

✦ Why use it
Without it, "minimal" quietly smuggles everything across the line and the MVP isn't minimal at all. It draws a real must-have vs nice-to-have line and gives you the reasoning to back every cut when someone pushes.

✦ How to use it
Just tell Agent Harry:
"Use the feature-prioritizer agent to score this backlog with RICE and draw the MVP cut line."

✦ Benefits
- RICE/ICE/Kano scores and a ranked list
- A real MVP cut line, not everything-in disguise
- A defensible reason behind every win, wait, and drop

The figma-designer turns an approved lo-fi layout into real hi-fi Figma frames. It reads the lo-fi handoff and the PRD, plugs in your Design System's actual components, fills in real content, applies your color and type tokens, and lays out the key states.

When a lo-fi layout has been approved and you want hi-fi Figma screens (not code) for the chosen flow. Or when you want every frame built from your real Design System, instance by instance.

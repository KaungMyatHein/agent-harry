✦ What it is
The prd-author turns your prioritized backlog into real PRDs — one per feature you tagged "in." It writes each spec to your project workspace and hands back a manifest table you confirm before any Deliver work starts.

✦ When to use it
When the Success-Metrics Gate has cleared and you have a prioritized backlog. Or when you want one PRD per "in"-tagged sub-feature, written consistently rather than by hand.

✦ Why use it
Without it, a "yes" stays a title nobody can build against. It's JTBD-driven and ruthless about scope — every PRD says what's in and, just as importantly, what's deliberately left out.

✦ How to use it
Just tell Agent Harry:

✦ Benefits
- One focused, JTBD-anchored PRD per "in"-tagged feature
- Specs written into your project workspace, versioned alongside everything
